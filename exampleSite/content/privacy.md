This site collects as little as it possibly can while still being a website.

## What we collect

**Nothing personally identifying.** There are no accounts, no forms that submit
anywhere, and no advertising.

**Aggregate page views**, if analytics are enabled. The theme supports
[Plausible](https://plausible.io/), which is cookieless and does not build a
profile of individual visitors. Google Analytics 4 is also supported for
organisations that require it; it is disabled by default.

## Cookies

This site sets **no cookies** of its own.

One exception: the visitor counter in the sidebar uses the browser's
`localStorage` to remember how many times *you* have visited, so it can
increment the displayed number. That value never leaves your browser and is not
sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data resets it.

## Third-party requests

By default: none. The theme loads no fonts, scripts, or stylesheets from any
external domain.

If we enable Plausible or Google Analytics, that adds exactly one third-party
request, and we say so in the config file where it's toggled.

## Your rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there's nothing to request, export, or
delete. If you've emailed us, we have your email — reply to any message and
ask, and we'll delete the thread.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what's collected, we'll note it in
the blog rather than quietly editing this page.
