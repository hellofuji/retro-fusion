---
title: "Retro Fusion"
description: "A small Hugo theme for people who miss the early web."
---

Retro Fusion is a Hugo theme inspired by early web culture: beveled borders,
tiled backgrounds, window chrome, and a fondness for pages that load before you
finish blinking.

It is deliberately small. There is one stylesheet, one small script, no build
step, and no third-party requests. If something you expected is missing, it
was probably left out on purpose — see [Design Notes](/posts/design-notes/).

Everything on this site is the documentation. Start with
[Getting Started](/posts/getting-started/), or browse
[all the docs](/posts/).
