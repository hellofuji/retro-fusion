We take on four projects a year, and we reply to every message — including the
ones where we're not the right fit.

## Email

**hello@example.org**

This is a real inbox read by a real person. Expect a reply within one working
day.

## What to include

Nothing formal. A sentence or two about what you're building is plenty to start:

- What the site is for
- Roughly when you'd like it live
- Whether you have existing content, or need help producing it

We'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit, and if it isn't, we'll usually
suggest someone who's better suited.

## Office hours

Monday to Thursday, 10:00–17:00 UTC. We don't do urgent, and we don't do
same-day turnarounds — but we do answer, every time, without a follow-up
sequence or a CRM drip campaign.

> **A note on forms:** this theme intentionally ships without a contact form
> backend. A form that silently drops submissions is worse than an email
> address. If you want a form, wire it to a service you control — or just use
> `mailto:`.
